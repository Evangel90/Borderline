## Forward fix

The agent now converts an exact supplied chunk ID quoted in a rationale into a structured citation, so the displayed source reference cannot be silently omitted from the citation field. The prompt also requires citations for named source-based claims, while allowing uncited actions grounded only in visible facts or synthetic drill instructions. A source-based or legal claim that remains uncited after retry is replaced by a cautious incident-based explanation. This change is prospective; it does not rewrite this run’s immutable audit log.

The citation audit is lexical and metadata-based. It cannot prove exact support or resolve legal authority, source translation, or applicability.
