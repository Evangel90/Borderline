# Source and claim coverage review

**Reviewed:** 1 November 2026. **Scope:** a twelve-case seed set from the completed synthetic run `retinopathy-rationale-20261101-1`.

`data/claim_review_seed.csv` records the exact decision, reviewed passage, assessment, and missing evidence for each case. Nine cases are marked `UNSUPPORTED` for their specific cited or invoked basis, one `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, and two `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED` for general topic relevance with unresolved applicability. These are manual passage checks for the recorded claims, not an overall accuracy estimate or a conclusion about current law.

Run `make evaluate-claims` to compare these labels with the current lexical triage. On this deliberately selected 12-case set, triage returned six `UNSUPPORTED`, three `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, and three `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`. One manually unsupported claim was labeled partly supported because a general clinical term overlapped a privacy passage; two others had no recorded citation and were labeled insufficient. This shows that the heuristic cannot replace exact claim-support review. The seed is not a representative sample, so no general accuracy rate is claimed.

The [DHA Standards for Sentinel Events Notification and Management in Health Facilities, version 1.1](https://dha.gov.ae/uploads/042025/Sentinel%20Events%20Notification%20And%20Management%20In%20Health%20Facilities-Final202541624.pdf) was archived in `data/raw/ae/` and registered with its SHA-256. Its scope covers serious-harm or death cases in DHA-licensed facilities. The synthetic scenario reports missed referrals but does not establish serious harm, so this source does not yet support classifying the incident as a sentinel event or asserting its notification procedure applies. Facility licensing status also needs confirmation.

The [DHA Clinical Governance Framework manual](https://dha.gov.ae/uploads/122024/Clinical%20Governance%20Framework%20Manual20241253268.pdf) was reviewed as a candidate but not ingested: its stated applicability lists hospitals and day surgical centres, while the scenario describes clinics without identifying either facility type.

The official English translation of [Federal Decree-Law No. 38 of 2024](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2751/download) is a promising EDE primary source. Its definition of medical equipment includes some AI-based diagnostic products; Articles 68 and 70 address product-safety reporting, investigation, and requests for information under stated conditions. This supports further evaluation of the simulated EDE role, but does not establish that RetinaVision-DX has the relevant UAE product classification, marketing status, incident category, or that every action in the drill follows from those provisions. The [official legislation page](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2751) states that the Arabic original controls interpretation. Automated download of that PDF returned HTTP 403 and the [MoHAP-hosted English PDF](https://mohap.gov.ae/en/w/federal-decree-law-no.-38-of-the-year-2024-concerning-medical-products-the-pharmacy-profession-and-pharmaceutical-establishments) returned a connection reset, so neither has been archived or indexed and no new EDE citation has been introduced into the pack.

Next: expand the seed to roughly 30–40 manually adjudicated questions; retrieve exact authoritative passages; verify institutional and incident scope; then measure passage retrieval and citation support. Do not count lexical triage as semantic validation.
